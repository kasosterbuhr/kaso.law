## Kaso Law Privacy Policy and SMS Terms & Conditions
Last Updated: July 11, 2026

Kaso Law respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you voluntarily provide when communicating with us. This policy outlines how we handle your data, particularly regarding SMS/text messaging.

## Part 1: Privacy Policy
Information We Collect: We may collect the following information when you voluntarily provide it: Name, mobile phone number, email address, message contents, and professional context (information related to scheduling, document review, and case coordination).
How We Use Your Information: Your information is strictly used for professional and administrative communication purposes, including responding to inquiries, scheduling meetings, following up on documents, and coordinating work matters.
Information Sharing & SMS Consent: Kaso Law does not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations or provide our professional services. CRITICAL PRIVACY DISCLOSURE REGARDING SMS: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.
Data Security: We utilize reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Part 2: SMS Terms & Conditions
By providing your phone number and opting in to receive SMS messages from Kaso Law, you agree to the following terms.

### Program Description:
Kaso Law utilizes a low-volume, two-way SMS program strictly for professional business communications, scheduling, document-review follow-ups, and responding to incoming inquiries. This program is not used for mass marketing or promotional messaging.

### Message Frequency:
Message frequency varies based on your ongoing interactions and case coordination with Kaso Law.

### Message and Data Rates:
Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

### Opt-Out Instructions:
You may cancel SMS messages at any time. To opt out, reply STOP to any text message from Kaso Law. After sending STOP, you will receive a single confirmation message stating that you have been unsubscribed. No further messages will be sent unless you explicitly opt in again. (Other supported keywords: STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT)
Help Instructions: If you need assistance, reply HELP to any message from Kaso Law. You may also request help by contacting us at the email or phone number below.
