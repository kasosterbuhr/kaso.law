## Part 1: Privacy Policy

Kaso Law respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you voluntarily provide when communicating with us, including through SMS/text messaging.

Kaso Law uses your information only for professional communication purposes, including:

### SMS Consent
By providing your mobile phone number and opting in to receive SMS messages from Kaso Law, you consent to receive text messages related to professional communications, scheduling, document-review follow-up, case-related coordination, replies to incoming inquiries, and general business communication.

### Information Sharing
Kaso Law does not sell or rent your personal information. Your information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:

* Protect the rights, safety, or security of Kaso Law or others.

*Note: Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.*

Kaso Law uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or loss. However, no method of electronic communication or storage is completely secure.
